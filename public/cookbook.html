Cheesy Chili Dog Casserole

From @manay.kitchen on Instagram — watch the original reel

Ingredients

  • 8 beef hot dogs
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 2 cans chili with beans
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. Wrap each hot dog individually in a flour tortilla.
  2. Arrange the wrapped hot dogs snugly in a baking dish.
  3. Pour both cans of chili over the top, covering everything.
  4. Sprinkle the shredded cheddar evenly over the chili.
  5. Bake at 375°F for 25 minutes, until hot, bubbly, and golden on top.
  6. Serve steaming.

Loaded Beef & Bean Baked Tortillas

From @mediterranean_bites_2026 on Instagram — watch the original reel

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1 cup refried beans
  • 6 small flour or corn tortillas
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded Mexican-blend or cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup diced red onion
  • Fresh cilantro, for garnish
  • Sour cream or Greek yogurt, for serving
  • Olive oil or cooking spray

Instructions

  1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat. Drain the grease, stir in the taco seasoning with a splash of water, and cook 2–3 minutes.
  2. Lay the tortillas on a baking sheet, brush or spray lightly with olive oil, and bake at 400°F for about 5 minutes until they start to crisp.
  3. Spread each tortilla with refried beans, then add seasoned beef and salsa. Top generously with shredded cheese.
  4. Return to the oven for 6–8 minutes, until the tortillas are crispy and the cheese is bubbling and melted.
  5. Finish with diced tomatoes, red onion, and fresh cilantro. Serve with sour cream or Greek yogurt.

Mini Chicken Pot Pies

From @quickmediterranean.recipes on Instagram — watch the original reel

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 1/2 small onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 sheet refrigerated puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten (for brushing)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F and grease a muffin tin.
  2. Melt the butter in a skillet and sauté the onion for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Stir in the flour and cook 1 minute. Slowly whisk in the chicken broth and milk until smooth and creamy.
  4. Add the chicken, peas, carrots, thyme, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Simmer 3–5 minutes.
  5. Cut the puff pastry into rounds and press them into the muffin cups.
  6. Fill each cup with the chicken mixture and cover with another pastry round. Pinch or press the edges to seal.
  7. Brush the tops with beaten egg and cut a small slit in each.
  8. Bake 20–25 minutes until golden and flaky. Cool a few minutes before serving.

Dorito Mexican Casserole (Crockpot)

From @easyrecipegram on Instagram — watch the original reel

Ingredients

  • 1 bag Doritos Nacho Cheese chips
  • 1 lb ground beef, cooked and crumbled
  • Taco seasoning (about 1 packet, divided)
  • Frozen corn
  • 2 cans Ro-Tel diced tomatoes with green chiles
  • 12 oz sour cream
  • 2 bags shredded Mexican-style cheese

Instructions

  1. Pour the whole bag of Doritos into a slow cooker and crush them down with a potato masher to form a base layer.
  2. Spread the cooked ground beef over the chips and season with taco seasoning.
  3. Layer in the frozen corn, both cans of Ro-Tel, and the sour cream, adding another sprinkle of taco seasoning between layers.
  4. Top everything with the shredded cheese.
  5. Cover and cook on HIGH for 3 hours, until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  6. Scoop into bowls and serve hot.

One-Pan Hamburger Dinner

From @shannieg on Instagram — watch the original reel

Note: the video didn't give exact quantities or oven temp/time, so this is the method as shown — season and size it to your household.

Ingredients

  • Hamburger patties (raw)
  • Sliced onions
  • Cream of mushroom soup (for a creamy sauce)
  • Beefy onion soup mix (1 packet)
  • Sliced mushrooms
  • Swiss cheese slices (one per patty)
  • Buns, for serving

Instructions

  1. Lay the raw hamburger patties in a glass baking dish and scatter sliced onions around and over them.
  2. Spread the mushroom soup over the patties as a sauce and sprinkle the onion soup mix over everything.
  3. Add the sliced mushrooms on top.
  4. Place a slice of Swiss cheese on each patty.
  5. Bake in a preheated oven until the patties are cooked through and the cheese is melted (about 350–375°F for 25–35 minutes is a reasonable starting point; check for doneness).
  6. Serve each patty on a bun with the onions, mushrooms, and sauce spooned over.

Lazy Chicken Parmesan Pasta

From Rebekah Garner on Facebook — watch the original reel

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 1 jar Alfredo sauce
  • 1 bag bow-tie pasta
  • Parmesan cheese, measured with your heart
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese, measured with your heart

Instructions

  1. Add the chicken breasts, marinara sauce, and Alfredo sauce to the Crock-Pot.
  2. Cover and cook on HIGH for about 3 hours, or until the chicken reaches 165°F.
  3. Shred the chicken directly in the sauce.
  4. Cook the bow-tie pasta separately according to the package directions, then drain.
  5. Stir the cooked pasta, Parmesan, and mozzarella into the Crock-Pot.
  6. Let everything warm together until the cheese is melted, then serve.

Crockpot Apple Cobbler

From Life With Mercedes on Facebook — watch the original reel

Ingredients

  • 2 cans apple pie filling
  • 1 tablespoon cinnamon sugar
  • 1 box white cake mix
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 stick butter, melted
  • Vanilla ice cream, for serving
  • Caramel syrup, for serving

Instructions

  1. Add the apple pie filling to the crockpot and sprinkle with the cinnamon sugar.
  2. Shake the 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon into the box of white cake mix, then pour the mix over the apples and flatten it out.
  3. Pour the melted butter over the top.
  4. Cover and cook on HIGH for 4 hours.
  5. Serve with vanilla ice cream and caramel syrup.

Peanut Butter Chocolate Banana Bark

From Crowded Kitchen on Facebook — watch the original reel

Note: the creator doesn't measure anything — amounts are to taste.

Ingredients

  • Bananas (about 3 overripe)
  • Peanut butter
  • Dark chocolate, melted
  • Salted peanuts (optional)

Instructions

  1. Smush the bananas flat (between two sheet pans works) into a nice, even layer.
  2. Spread peanut butter over the top.
  3. Freeze until the peanut butter is solid.
  4. Pour melted dark chocolate over the top and add the peanuts immediately, before the chocolate sets.
  5. Once set, chop into bite-sized pieces. Store in the freezer.

Viral Butterball Crockpot Turkey

From Allrecipes on Facebook — watch the original reel

Note: the video didn't state exact amounts for the seasonings or the cranberry sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 Butterball turkey breast (with gravy packet), netting left on
  • Salt and pepper
  • Onion powder
  • Garlic powder
  • Poultry seasoning
  • 1 packet onion soup mix
  • Whole berry cranberry sauce
  • 1/2 cup liquid — apple cider and orange juice combined (the video used all apple cider)
  • Fresh rosemary
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons cornstarch, plus a couple of tablespoons cold water

Instructions

  1. Put the turkey breast in the crockpot with the netting still on, so it stays together.
  2. Season with salt, pepper, onion powder, garlic powder, and poultry seasoning.
  3. Add the onion soup mix and the cranberry sauce, pour the cider/juice over the top, and add the fresh rosemary.
  4. Cook on HIGH for 4 hours.
  5. Take the turkey out and let it rest.
  6. Pour the cooking juices into a pan, stir in the gravy packet, and heat. Whisk the cornstarch with the cold water and stir it in to thicken the gravy.
  7. Slice the turkey and serve with the gravy.

Meatball Pasta Bake

From Amy Darley on Facebook — watch the original reel

Note: the post lists the ingredients without quantities.

Ingredients

  • Uncooked rotini pasta
  • Water
  • Pasta sauce
  • Frozen meatballs
  • Kosher salt
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F and grease a baking dish.
  2. Add the uncooked pasta, water, pasta sauce, meatballs, and salt to the dish. Cover with foil.
  3. Bake 35 minutes, stirring halfway through.
  4. Remove the foil, top with mozzarella, and bake another 5 to 8 minutes until melted and bubbly.
