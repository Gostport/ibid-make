Tonight's Blossom word

SACALAIT

/sækəˈleɪ/  ·  sack-uh-LAY  ·  noun · Louisiana French: "sack of milk"

The Definition, Translated

Sacalait — the white crappie (Pomoxis annularis), as south Louisiana calls it — the silvery panfish so prized for its sweet white flesh that Louisiana made it the official state freshwater fish.

On the surface the name is French sac à lait, "a sack of milk," for a fish whose belly flashes milky-white in the sun. But that French meaning is folk etymology — the word is ultimately from Choctaw sakli, "trout." A Choctaw fish wearing a French milk-sack for a coat.

Say it right: the final t is silent, and the first syllable rhymes with "rock," not "back." In north Louisiana the same fish is called "white perch."

"SACALAIT, m. Sac a lait, 'milk bag,' is the Louisiana name for the Crappie… a name suggested by the silvery olive appearance of the fish, or by its beautiful white flesh." — William A. Read, Louisiana-French (1931)

What It Is

One fish, two names, and an older truth underneath:

I. The Fish

The white crappie, Pomoxis annularis — the most desirable panfish in Louisiana water, and the state's official freshwater fish, prized for its sweet white flesh.

II. The French Story

A fresh-caught fish flashes opalescent silver-white, like a little bag of milk in its belly. Folk taxonomy: name the thing by the picture it makes in the sun.

III. The Older Truth

Choctaw sakli, "trout." The name was already swimming in Louisiana before the French gave it a milk-sack — they just heard it and dressed it in French.

A Short History of a Fish in a Milk-Sack

Before the French — Sakli, "Trout"

Long before anyone said sac à lait, the Choctaw word sakli — "trout" — named fish in these waters. The French name came later, laid over the older word like a coat that happened to fit.

1931 — Read Writes It Down

William A. Read's Louisiana-French records the word, notes the silent final t, and explains the milk-bag story. English speakers, he observed, say it roughly "sackalay."

State Fish — Official Business

The legislature declared the white crappie Louisiana's official state freshwater fish — the sacalait, honored in law under its English name, still swimming under its Choctaw one.
